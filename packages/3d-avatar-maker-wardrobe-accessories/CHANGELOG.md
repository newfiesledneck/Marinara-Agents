# 3D Avatar Maker: Accessories release notes

## 0.2.04 — 2026-11-23

- Every piece is fitted again, placed as MakeHuman places it, so socks, gloves, hats and jewellery keep the shape they have in their own pictures.

## 0.2.03 — 2026-11-23

- 50 new pieces for the slots 3D Avatar Maker 0.1.10 added. Jewellery: four bracelets and bangles, five anklets, four necklaces and a spiked collar, three pairs of earrings, ear and face piercings, and four for the belly. Six kinds of horns and antlers, seven pairs of wings and two tails. Things to hold or carry: a sword, a microphone, a tennis racket, a shopping bag, a shoulder bag, a sling purse and an electric guitar. Also a headset, a head mirror, a stethoscope and two tactical vests.
- Horns, wings, tails, held things, earrings and the solid bracelets and anklets are fixed to one bone, so they keep their shape as the avatar moves.

## 0.2.02 — 2026-11-23

- 14 hair styles for the Hair tab, all by Elvaerwyn and CC BY 4.0: a 1950s updo, a braided bun, long straight, loose, side-parted, swept-back and tousled styles, two half-updos, a feathered cut to the shoulders, a layered cut with a fringe, a short straight cut, a wavy bob and a short swept-up men's cut. The long and loose ones swing as the avatar moves. They need 3D Avatar Maker 0.1.10 or later; an older maker still shows the rest of the pack.

## 0.2.01 — 2026-11-23

- 2 new pieces: a feather headdress and a lace choker (in the Necklace slot). Needs 3D Avatar Maker 0.1.10 or later, which added that slot.

## 0.2.00 — 2026-11-23

- The hats, hoods, veil, headband, top hat, pith helmet and brass circlet from the retired Historical and Fantasy and Modern Casual packs are here now, with the same names, so saved avatars still find them. Clothes went to the new 3D Avatar Maker: Clothes pack.
- 15 new pieces: a Santa hat, a chef's hat, a fishing hat, a patrol cap, a sun visor, a swim cap, a steel helmet, an aero cycling helmet, a riding helmet, safety glasses, swimming goggles, sports sunglasses, a respirator mask, a gas mask and lace-top stockings.
- 24 of the 45 pieces are CC BY now. A saved avatar credits the maker of every CC BY piece it wears.

## 0.1.01 — 2026-11-23

- Took out the cat mask, the hero mask and the lightning earrings. Their model files say AGPL-3, which doesn't match the CC0 their catalogue gives, so they are left out like other such items. 19 pieces remain.

## 0.1.00 — 2026-11-23

- First version: 22 accessories for 3D Avatar Maker's Clothes tab: six pairs of glasses, two eye masks, a medical mask, pearl and lightning earrings, two rings, three lengths of gloves, four kinds of socks and leg warmers, a cloche hat and a Christmas hat.
- Every piece is from the MakeHuman community and free to share (CC0). Needs 3D Avatar Maker 0.1.04 or later.
