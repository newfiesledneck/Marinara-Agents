# 3D Avatar Maker: Accessories

95 accessories for [3D Avatar Maker](../3d-avatar-maker/README.md): hats, caps and helmets, hoods, a veil and a
headband, a brass circlet, glasses, goggles and sunglasses, masks, earrings and piercings, rings, bracelets,
anklets and necklaces, belly jewellery, gloves, socks and stockings; horns and antlers, wings and tails; and things
to hold or carry: a sword, a microphone, a tennis racket, bags and an electric guitar, with a headset, a head
mirror, a stethoscope and tactical vests. They come from the MakeHuman community. Most are CC BY, so they are free
to share as long as their makers are credited. The maker does that for you: a saved avatar names the maker of
every CC BY piece it wears. The rest are CC0.

Since 0.2.00 this pack also holds the headwear of the retired Historical and Fantasy and Modern Casual packs,
under the same names. Their clothes are in [3D Avatar Maker: Clothes](../3d-avatar-maker-wardrobe-clothes/README.md).

This package holds data only. It has no Home tab, no code and no permissions. It needs **3D Avatar Maker
0.1.10 or later**, which added the slots for necklaces, wrists, ankles, the belly, horns, the back, tails, the
hip and the hands, and can fix a piece to one bone. The maker finds it when both are installed.

## Use it

1. Install **3D Avatar Maker** and this package from **Agents → Agent Organizer**. Neither needs a restart.
2. Open **Home → 3D Avatar Maker** and go to the **Clothes** tab. The pieces below appear beside the maker's
   own, in the slot each is worn in. Under a piece, a line names the pack, its maker and its licence.

A piece is fitted to the maker's bodies as of 3D Avatar Maker 0.1.02, and follows their build sliders. A
maker whose bodies have changed since then leaves the pack out until the pack is fitted again.

"Any colour" pieces are plain, and the maker dyes them. The others keep their own picture, which a colour of
your own tints. Gloves hide a ring and the bracelets, and socks hide the toenails and the anklets; socks go under
any shoes. A scarf hides a necklace, and a one-piece outfit hides what's on the belly. Most hats and helmets hide
the hair and any horns; the headband, the circlet and the sun visor sit over the hair, and the gas mask covers
the whole head. The glasses' lenses are clear, except the sunglasses'.

Horns, antlers, wings, tails, earrings, the bracelets and anklets, the headset, the head mirror and everything
held are fixed to one bone, so they keep their shape as the avatar moves: a sword turns with the hand, wings
with the upper back. The bracelets are made for one wrist and the anklets for the left ankle, as their makers
modelled them. A held thing sits in an open hand, as the maker has no grip yet.

## How it's made

`packages/3d-avatar-maker/assets/packs.json` lists the pieces. `assets/scripts/make_mh_packs.py` fits them to
the bodies in Blender the way the maker's own MakeHuman clothes are fitted, and
`scripts/build-3d-avatar-maker-packs.mjs` packs them into `wardrobe/*.pack.png`, writes `avatar-pack.json`
with each piece's credit, and builds this package.
