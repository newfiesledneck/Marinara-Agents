# 3D Avatar Maker: Clothes release notes

## 0.1.01 — 2026-11-23

- Every piece is fitted again, closer to how it looks in its own picture: placed as MakeHuman places it, it keeps its shape on the maker's bodies. Skirts and dresses hang smooth and whole, with no strip pulled out at the back and no split between the legs, and the lumps and jagged hems are gone.
- Pieces no longer follow the arms unless they cover them, so a skirt isn't dragged when the arms come down.
- 8 more pieces that the first fitting couldn't place cleanly: four floor-length gowns, a skinsuit, a tracksuit hoodie dress, a wetsuit and men's monk-strap shoes.

## 0.1.00 — 2026-11-23

- First version: 178 pieces of clothing for 3D Avatar Maker's Clothes tab in one pack. It replaces four packs: Everyday Women's Clothes, Everyday Men's Clothes and Suits, Historical and Fantasy Clothes, and Modern Casual Clothes. Their 75 garments are here with the same names, so saved avatars still find them; their hats and other headwear moved to the Accessories pack.
- 103 new pieces: 28 dresses, a suit, a jumpsuit and three sporty one-pieces, 13 tops, 17 pairs of trousers and shorts including swimming trunks, 5 skirts, 27 pieces of underwear and swimwear, and 8 pairs of shoes.
- Every piece is from the MakeHuman community. Most are CC BY, and a saved avatar credits the maker of every CC BY piece it wears. Needs 3D Avatar Maker 0.1.03 or later.
