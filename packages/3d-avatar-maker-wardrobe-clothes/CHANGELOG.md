# 3D Avatar Maker: Clothes release notes

## 0.1.02 — 2026-11-23

- 22 skirts, dresses, gowns, robes and coats now hang from the hips and swing as one piece as the avatar moves, instead of following each leg. Needs 3D Avatar Maker 0.1.11 or later; an older one shows them as before.

## 0.1.01 — 2026-11-23

- Bras, bikini tops, briefs and bikini bottoms are underwear now: the maker offers them in its Underwear lists in place of the body's own, under any shirt, skirt or trousers. Before, they took the Shirt or top and Legs slots, so nothing could be worn over them. Needs 3D Avatar Maker 0.1.11 or later; an older one leaves the underwear out.
- 8 new pieces: a bra with a bow, a lace camisole, briefs with a bow, embroidered briefs, a lace thong, a strappy lace thong, a lace teddy and a suspender belt (worn in the Belt slot).
- Swimsuits, bodysuits, the shapewear, the teddy and the men's swimming trunks say they are worn on bare skin, so the maker leaves the underwear off under them.

## 0.1.00 — 2026-11-23

- First version: 178 pieces of clothing for 3D Avatar Maker's Clothes tab in one pack. It replaces four packs: Everyday Women's Clothes, Everyday Men's Clothes and Suits, Historical and Fantasy Clothes, and Modern Casual Clothes. Their 75 garments are here with the same names, so saved avatars still find them; their hats and other headwear moved to the Accessories pack.
- 103 new pieces: 28 dresses, a suit, a jumpsuit and three sporty one-pieces, 13 tops, 17 pairs of trousers and shorts including swimming trunks, 5 skirts, 27 pieces of underwear and swimwear, and 8 pairs of shoes.
- Every piece is from the MakeHuman community. Most are CC BY, and a saved avatar credits the maker of every CC BY piece it wears. Needs 3D Avatar Maker 0.1.03 or later.
