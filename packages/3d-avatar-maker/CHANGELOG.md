# 3D Avatar Maker release notes

## 0.1.14 — 2026-11-23

- A new **Generate** tab lets a model on one of your connections design the avatar from a character's or persona's card, and their portrait if you tick it. **Revise avatar** sends the avatar as it looks now, with what you'd like changed, and asks only for what to change. **Undo** puts the last look back.
- It's off until you turn it on, says what each call sends, and needs no restart. Everything else works without it.
## 0.1.13 — 2026-11-23

- Clothes keep the shape they have in their own pictures: fitted where MakeHuman places them, skirts hang whole with no strip pulled out or seam between the legs, and pieces no longer follow the arms unless they cover them. Each layer is worn just over the one under it, so leggings and socks stop showing through.
- **Show underwear under clothes** now starts on.
- Rebuilt beards and moustache, a new mutton chops style, and stubble painted on the skin in five shapes with its own colour and density. The skin under a beard or hair is shaded in the hair's colour.
- Arms push the bust: folded or crossed arms lift or press it, and it moulds round a hand in the preview.

## 0.1.12 — 2026-11-23

- The Brow and On the back places say what they hold now that the Accessories pack fills them: face piercings and a head mirror, and wings or a guitar on its strap. Install Accessories 0.2.03 for jewellery, horns and antlers, wings, tails, and things to hold or carry.

## 0.1.11 — 2026-11-23

- Skirts, dresses, robes, coats and cloaks hang from the hips and swing as one piece, and the bust moves a little, clothes with it. A saved VRM swings them too.
- An **Animation** list plays the avatar walking, dancing, talking, waving, sitting and more, mostly Quaternius's CC0 clips. Preview only; saved files rest as before.
- Makeup on any skin: eyeshadow, eyeliner, blush and lipstick in your own colours, or one of seven ready-made looks. The made-up duplicate skins are gone; looks that used one keep its makeup.
- Pack underwear goes in the Underwear lists. **Show underwear under clothes** keeps it on under clothes; it starts off, as clothes aren't fitted around underwear yet.
- Six new bobs in the Hair tab.
- Fixed: the eyes rolled up under the lids when looking at the camera from close or above.

## 0.1.10 — 2026-11-23

- Room for accessories fixed to the body: things held in either hand, horns or antlers, a necklace, something on the back, at the hip, on the wrists, ankles and belly, and a tail. A piece held or fixed this way keeps its shape as the avatar moves. The packs that fill these places come next.
- Wardrobe packs can now add hair styles too, which the Hair tab lists beside the maker's own.
- An avatar that wears something from a pack you've since uninstalled keeps it in its look. The maker says what isn't shown and which pack to install, and it comes back once the pack is installed again. A saved file still holds only what's worn.

## 0.1.09 — 2026-11-23

- A lip colour you pick stays on the lips. It used to reach a little past them onto the skin round the mouth; now it fades in softly from the lips' edge, the way the made-up skins paint their lips.

## 0.1.08 — 2026-11-23

- Hair moves: the long, ponytail, braid, bob and angled bob styles swing as the avatar moves, and settle back into place when it stands still. In a VRM, the 3D Avatars agent and other VRM apps swing them. The head, neck, shoulders, arms and back keep the hair out.
- A **Moving** pose in the maker's pose list turns the head now and then, so you can see the hair swing. A tail swings in the maker too.

## 0.1.07 — 2026-11-23

- The face moves: avatars blink, shape their mouth as they talk, and look happy, angry, sad, relaxed or surprised. In a chat, the 3D Avatars agent plays these on both VRM and GLB files.
- Teeth and a tongue inside the mouth, seen when it opens.
- The eyes turn to look. In a VRM, the 3D Avatars agent and other VRM apps move them.
- A **Face** list under the picture plays it all in the maker: blinking and looking at you, talking, or one expression held.

## 0.1.06 — 2026-11-23

- Save as VRM: the Save tab now saves a `.vrm` file as well as a `.glb`. Import it in 3D Avatars as a VRM model. It stands in a T-pose and works in other VRM apps too. A tail swings as the avatar moves.
- The VRM's terms: put your name as the author, and choose who may use the avatar, commercial use, sharing and changes, and credit. Your choices are kept for the next avatar you save. A VRM wearing credited pack clothes always asks for credit to their makers.
- **Open** reads a saved `.vrm` back for editing, as it does a `.glb`.

## 0.1.05 — 2026-11-23

- Took out two skins, Mid-toned and Viking tattoos. Their material files say AGPL-3, which doesn't match the CC0 their catalogue gives. An avatar saved with one of them opens with the default skin instead.

## 0.1.04 — 2026-11-23

- New places to wear things: glasses, a face mask, earrings, rings and socks. The new Accessories pack fills them, and adds gloves and two hats.
- Gloves hide rings, and socks hide the toenails, the way gloves and shoes already hide the nails.

## 0.1.03 — 2026-11-23

- Wardrobe packs: install a 3D Avatar Maker wardrobe pack and its clothes appear in the Clothes tab, each with a line saying which pack it's from and who made it. The first two are Everyday Women's Clothes and Everyday Men's Clothes and Suits.
- A saved avatar names the makers of any credited pack clothes it wears.

## 0.1.02 — 2026-11-23

- Real nails on every finger and toe: short, medium, long or none, and toenails on or off, in any colour. Gloves and shoes hide them.
- 23 more skins, among them freckled, ginger, bronzed, Eurasian, tattooed and made-up ones.
- 15 more eye pictures: amber, hazel, sapphire and other natural colours, cat's eyes, reptile and dragon eyes, and a trial anime eye.
- Four brow styles and two lash styles with fine, natural hairs.
- 29 more sliders for the cheeks, ears, nose, arms and hands, such as jowls, a bulbous nose and knobbly knuckles. A new avatar starts with truer ears, nose and hands.

## 0.1.01 — 2026-11-23

- Nails are now a part of their own on every finger and toe, so a nail colour has a clean edge.
- 46 new face sliders in eight groups (eyes, brows, nose, mouth, jaw and chin, cheeks, ears, head), with ready-made shapes for each, such as hooded eyes, a Roman nose or an underbite.
- 15 new body sliders for the arms, legs, torso and hips, and her bust.
- Races are presets on the same body: choosing one sets race sliders you can mix and adjust, plus height and limbs. Horns, tusks, a tail and beards are accessories anyone can wear.
- A first Anime proportions style, and clean-edged lip colour.

## 0.1.00 — 2026-11-22

- First version: a Home tab where you build a 3D avatar for a character or persona and save it as a GLB file for the 3D Avatars agent.
- Male and female bodies in eight races, with build, face and proportion sliders, three ages, skin and eye pictures or colours of your own, 14 hair styles, and 78 pieces of clothing and armour.
- Save the look as a small file, or open a saved avatar again to keep editing it.
- The face doesn't move yet, so in a chat the avatar nods while it talks.
