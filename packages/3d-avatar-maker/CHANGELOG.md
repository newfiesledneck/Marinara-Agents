# 3D Avatar Maker release notes

## 0.1.07 — 2026-11-23

- The face moves: avatars blink, shape their mouth as they talk, and look happy, angry, sad, relaxed or surprised. In a chat, the 3D Avatars agent plays these on both VRM and GLB files.
- Teeth and a tongue inside the mouth, seen when it opens.
- The eyes turn to look. In a VRM, the 3D Avatars agent and other VRM apps move them.
- A **Face** list under the picture plays it all in the maker: blinking and looking at you, talking, or one expression held.

## 0.1.06 — 2026-11-23

- Save as VRM: the Save tab now saves a `.vrm` file as well as a `.glb`. Import it in 3D Avatars as a VRM model. It stands in a T-pose and works in other VRM apps too. A tail swings as the avatar moves.
- The VRM's terms: put your name as the author, and choose who may use the avatar, commercial use, sharing and changes, and credit. Your choices are kept for the next avatar you save. A VRM wearing credited pack clothes always asks for credit to their makers.
- **Open** reads a saved `.vrm` back for editing, as it does a `.glb`.

## 0.1.05 — 2026-11-23

- Took out two skins, Mid-toned and Viking tattoos. Their material files say AGPL-3, which doesn't match the CC0 their catalogue gives. An avatar saved with one of them opens with the default skin instead.

## 0.1.04 — 2026-11-23

- New places to wear things: glasses, a face mask, earrings, rings and socks. The new Accessories pack fills them, and adds gloves and two hats.
- Gloves hide rings, and socks hide the toenails, the way gloves and shoes already hide the nails.

## 0.1.03 — 2026-11-23

- Wardrobe packs: install a 3D Avatar Maker wardrobe pack and its clothes appear in the Clothes tab, each with a line saying which pack it's from and who made it. The first two are Everyday Women's Clothes and Everyday Men's Clothes and Suits.
- A saved avatar names the makers of any credited pack clothes it wears.

## 0.1.02 — 2026-11-23

- Real nails on every finger and toe: short, medium, long or none, and toenails on or off, in any colour. Gloves and shoes hide them.
- 23 more skins, among them freckled, ginger, bronzed, Eurasian, tattooed and made-up ones.
- 15 more eye pictures: amber, hazel, sapphire and other natural colours, cat's eyes, reptile and dragon eyes, and a trial anime eye.
- Four brow styles and two lash styles with fine, natural hairs.
- 29 more sliders for the cheeks, ears, nose, arms and hands, such as jowls, a bulbous nose and knobbly knuckles. A new avatar starts with truer ears, nose and hands.

## 0.1.01 — 2026-11-23

- Nails are now a part of their own on every finger and toe, so a nail colour has a clean edge.
- 46 new face sliders in eight groups (eyes, brows, nose, mouth, jaw and chin, cheeks, ears, head), with ready-made shapes for each, such as hooded eyes, a Roman nose or an underbite.
- 15 new body sliders for the arms, legs, torso and hips, and her bust.
- Races are presets on the same body: choosing one sets race sliders you can mix and adjust, plus height and limbs. Horns, tusks, a tail and beards are accessories anyone can wear.
- A first Anime proportions style, and clean-edged lip colour.

## 0.1.00 — 2026-11-22

- First version: a Home tab where you build a 3D avatar for a character or persona and save it as a GLB file for the 3D Avatars agent.
- Male and female bodies in eight races, with build, face and proportion sliders, three ages, skin and eye pictures or colours of your own, 14 hair styles, and 78 pieces of clothing and armour.
- Save the look as a small file, or open a saved avatar again to keep editing it.
- The face doesn't move yet, so in a chat the avatar nods while it talks.
