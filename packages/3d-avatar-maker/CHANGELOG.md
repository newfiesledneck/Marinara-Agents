# 3D Avatar Maker release notes

## 0.1.30 — 2026-11-28

- Eight beards for the Realistic body, made of separate strands like its hair: short beard, full beard, long beard, goatee, moustache, walrus moustache, chin curtain and mutton chops. Choose one under **Facial hair** on the Hair tab, in the hair's colour or one of its own.
- A beard moves with the face: the chin's part comes down as the mouth opens, and a moustache stays on the lip. The skin under it is painted in its colour, so none shows between the hairs.
- It lies over the face, neck and chest as you shape them, as the hair does. Painted stubble still works, alone or under a beard.
- Generate and Revise avatar can choose a beard and its colour for the Realistic body.

## 0.1.29 — 2026-11-27

- Sixteen new hair styles for the Realistic body, made of separate strands like its first four: buzz cut, short crop, pixie, side part, undercut, mohawk, afro, curly to the shoulders, straight to the shoulders, long with a fringe, long and wavy, ponytail, low ponytail, braid, bun and twin tails.
- The long ones swing and lie over the shoulders and chest as the other strand styles do. The tails and the braid swing as one.
- The hair is a little less shiny.

## 0.1.28 — 2026-11-27

- The Realistic body's hair is draped over the body. On a build with wider shoulders, a fuller chest or more weight, long hair used to pass through the skin; it now lies on the shoulders, chest and back and hangs on from there, in the preview and in a saved file.
- In motion the hair meets the shoulders, upper arms, chest and breasts it rests on, where before it swung through whatever it lay against. It is also a little stiffer, so it comes back to how it lies.
- With the hair's Movement slider at "Held still" the hair meets nothing and stays exactly as it lies.

## 0.1.27 — 2026-11-27

- The Realistic body has 146 shape sliders, up from 39, in groups on its Body and Face tabs: build, chest and shoulders, waist and hips, arms and hands, legs and feet, neck; head shape, forehead and brow, eyes, nose, cheeks, mouth, chin and jaw, ears.
- New among them: eyelid shapes (monolid, hooded, crease, how far each lid opens), eye bags, brow ridge, nose bridge, tip and width, lip shapes, chin cleft, jaw corners, ear shapes, skull shape, hand and foot size, fingernail and toenail length, and six more regional feature sets.
- A **Fantasy** group: pointed ears, slit pupils, fangs, sharp teeth, a forked tongue and no nose.
- Two sliders are renamed to say what they do: Nose length (how long the nose is) and Nose projection (how far it stands out).

## 0.1.26 — 2026-11-27

- The Realistic body's hair swings, and its bust and buttocks move a little as the body does, in the preview and in a saved VRM. The long styles swing, the bob moves at its ends, and Slicked back stays on the head.
- The buttocks move on the Male and Female bodies too, and tight clothes over them move with them.
- Each moving part has its own **Movement** controls, on the Body tab (bust, buttocks) and the Hair tab (hair): a tick box that turns it off, and a slider from held still to twice as far. A part turned off leaves its spring bones out of the saved file.

## 0.1.25 — 2026-11-27

- The Realistic body has makeup on its Skin and eyes tab: eyeshadow, eyeliner, blush and lipstick, each a colour and a strength, with the presets the other bodies have.
- It has nail colour for fingernails and toenails, and stubble on its Hair tab: light or heavy stubble, a moustache or goatee shadow, or a short beard, in the hair's colour or its own.
- Its **Painted underwear** choice is remembered in this browser. A new or generated avatar starts the way you last left it, where before it was switched back on.
- **Generate** and **Revise avatar** send the model only the options of the body the avatar has, and keep that body. A design used to choose between the Male and Female bodies itself; now choose the body on the Body tab first.

## 0.1.24 — 2026-11-27

- The Realistic body has eye colours on its Skin and eyes tab: any colour on its own iris, or one of the 24 eye pictures the other bodies have.
- It has eyelashes, which close with the eyes, and fourteen brow styles on its Hair tab. The brows are the hair's colour unless you give them their own; the lashes are the brows' colour, darker.
- Its hair is darker at the roots than at the tips. It was the other way round.
- Generate and Revise avatar can choose its eye colour or picture, its brow style and its brow colour.

## 0.1.23 — 2026-11-27

- **Generate** and **Revise avatar** work on the Realistic body: the model is given that body's own sliders, skin tone and hair styles, and the avatar stays the Realistic body. Before, generating swapped it for the Male or Female body.
- The paint under the Realistic body's hair starts a little inside the hairline, so no painted band shows on the forehead or at the temples.

## 0.1.22 — 2026-11-27

- The Realistic body's scalp is painted in the hair's colour where a style's hair grows, up to its hairline, so skin no longer shows between the strands from the side, above or behind.
- The patchy, blocky edge that showed at the temples and hairline is gone.

## 0.1.21 — 2026-11-27

- The Realistic body's painted underwear for a female build is a strapless bra, sitting close under the breasts, and cheeky panties that end where the legs meet. They no longer run down the inner thighs.

## 0.1.20 — 2026-11-27

- The Realistic body wears underwear painted on its skin: panties and a bra on a female build, boxer briefs on a male one. It is part of the skin's picture, so nothing shows through it in any pose.
- A **Painted underwear** tick box on its Clothes tab takes it off, for bare skin as the model has it. It replaces the blurred patch 0.1.19 had between the legs.

## 0.1.19 — 2026-11-27

- A third body to choose on the Body tab: **Realistic body (new, in progress)**. It is a different base model with scanned-looking skin, its own eyes, teeth and brows, and the same blinks, mouth shapes and feelings. It plays every preview animation and saves as a VRM or GLB like the others.
- It has 39 shape sliders of its own (a male-to-female slider, build, age, and the face), a skin tone slider from light to dark, and four hair styles made of thousands of separate strands.
- The Male and Female bodies are unchanged and keep everything made for them. The Realistic body has no clothes, underwear, eyelashes, eye colours or makeup yet, and its hair doesn't swing yet.

## 0.1.18 — 2026-11-26

- The preview is much lighter on the graphics card. It was drawn as often as the screen asked, 120 times a second and more on many screens; it's now drawn 30 times a second at rest and 60 while you turn the view or an animation plays.
- Fixed: every change to the avatar left textures and buffers on the graphics card until the page was closed, so a long session kept using more.

## 0.1.17 — 2026-11-26

- Skirts and dresses hang from the waist and move as one piece of cloth: a leg lifts the cloth over it and the cloth beside it follows, walking, dancing, sitting and crouching. A thigh no longer shows at the hip, and pleated skirts no longer crumple.
- An open mouth shows the upper teeth and little of the lower, where it showed a full lower row and no upper teeth. The teeth are ivory in place of grey.
- Known: the kit's own Long skirt is made of few, large panels, so a lifted thigh can still cut through it. Skirts from the Clothes pack don't have this.

## 0.1.16 — 2026-11-26

- The Animation list starts with **In a chat**: the avatar waiting, talking, thinking and listening as Marinara Engine shows it. A chat plays no recorded animation, so these four are what you'll see there.
- Skin, underwear and socks that lie directly under clothes aren't drawn or saved, so they stop coming through as the avatar moves. Legs now show under skirts and dresses, and the chest in an open collar.
- Skirts, dresses and coats are carried by the legs under them and move as one piece, with sixteen chains round in place of eight. First pass: a deeply pleated skirt still crumples when sitting.
- A gentler **Happy** face, and a calmer **Relaxed** one.
- Fixed: fingers curled through the palm in most animations.
- Fixed: the sitting animation threw both hands up halfway through.

## 0.1.15 — 2026-11-23

- Fixed: reopening the maker went back to the free draft instead of the character or persona you were last working on.

## 0.1.14 — 2026-11-23

- A new **Generate** tab lets a model on one of your connections design the avatar from a character's or persona's card, and their portrait if you tick it. **Revise avatar** sends the avatar as it looks now, with what you'd like changed, and asks only for what to change. **Undo** puts the last look back.
- It's off until you turn it on, says what each call sends, and needs no restart. Everything else works without it.
## 0.1.13 — 2026-11-23

- Clothes keep the shape they have in their own pictures: fitted where MakeHuman places them, skirts hang whole with no strip pulled out or seam between the legs, and pieces no longer follow the arms unless they cover them. Each layer is worn just over the one under it, so leggings and socks stop showing through.
- **Show underwear under clothes** now starts on.
- Rebuilt beards and moustache, a new mutton chops style, and stubble painted on the skin in five shapes with its own colour and density. The skin under a beard or hair is shaded in the hair's colour.
- Arms push the bust: folded or crossed arms lift or press it, and it moulds round a hand in the preview.

## 0.1.12 — 2026-11-23

- The Brow and On the back places say what they hold now that the Accessories pack fills them: face piercings and a head mirror, and wings or a guitar on its strap. Install Accessories 0.2.03 for jewellery, horns and antlers, wings, tails, and things to hold or carry.

## 0.1.11 — 2026-11-23

- Skirts, dresses, robes, coats and cloaks hang from the hips and swing as one piece, and the bust moves a little, clothes with it. A saved VRM swings them too.
- An **Animation** list plays the avatar walking, dancing, talking, waving, sitting and more, mostly Quaternius's CC0 clips. Preview only; saved files rest as before.
- Makeup on any skin: eyeshadow, eyeliner, blush and lipstick in your own colours, or one of seven ready-made looks. The made-up duplicate skins are gone; looks that used one keep its makeup.
- Pack underwear goes in the Underwear lists. **Show underwear under clothes** keeps it on under clothes; it starts off, as clothes aren't fitted around underwear yet.
- Six new bobs in the Hair tab.
- Fixed: the eyes rolled up under the lids when looking at the camera from close or above.

## 0.1.10 — 2026-11-23

- Room for accessories fixed to the body: things held in either hand, horns or antlers, a necklace, something on the back, at the hip, on the wrists, ankles and belly, and a tail. A piece held or fixed this way keeps its shape as the avatar moves. The packs that fill these places come next.
- Wardrobe packs can now add hair styles too, which the Hair tab lists beside the maker's own.
- An avatar that wears something from a pack you've since uninstalled keeps it in its look. The maker says what isn't shown and which pack to install, and it comes back once the pack is installed again. A saved file still holds only what's worn.

## 0.1.09 — 2026-11-23

- A lip colour you pick stays on the lips. It used to reach a little past them onto the skin round the mouth; now it fades in softly from the lips' edge, the way the made-up skins paint their lips.

## 0.1.08 — 2026-11-23

- Hair moves: the long, ponytail, braid, bob and angled bob styles swing as the avatar moves, and settle back into place when it stands still. In a VRM, the 3D Avatars agent and other VRM apps swing them. The head, neck, shoulders, arms and back keep the hair out.
- A **Moving** pose in the maker's pose list turns the head now and then, so you can see the hair swing. A tail swings in the maker too.

## 0.1.07 — 2026-11-23

- The face moves: avatars blink, shape their mouth as they talk, and look happy, angry, sad, relaxed or surprised. In a chat, the 3D Avatars agent plays these on both VRM and GLB files.
- Teeth and a tongue inside the mouth, seen when it opens.
- The eyes turn to look. In a VRM, the 3D Avatars agent and other VRM apps move them.
- A **Face** list under the picture plays it all in the maker: blinking and looking at you, talking, or one expression held.

## 0.1.06 — 2026-11-23

- Save as VRM: the Save tab now saves a `.vrm` file as well as a `.glb`. Import it in 3D Avatars as a VRM model. It stands in a T-pose and works in other VRM apps too. A tail swings as the avatar moves.
- The VRM's terms: put your name as the author, and choose who may use the avatar, commercial use, sharing and changes, and credit. Your choices are kept for the next avatar you save. A VRM wearing credited pack clothes always asks for credit to their makers.
- **Open** reads a saved `.vrm` back for editing, as it does a `.glb`.

## 0.1.05 — 2026-11-23

- Took out two skins, Mid-toned and Viking tattoos. Their material files say AGPL-3, which doesn't match the CC0 their catalogue gives. An avatar saved with one of them opens with the default skin instead.

## 0.1.04 — 2026-11-23

- New places to wear things: glasses, a face mask, earrings, rings and socks. The new Accessories pack fills them, and adds gloves and two hats.
- Gloves hide rings, and socks hide the toenails, the way gloves and shoes already hide the nails.

## 0.1.03 — 2026-11-23

- Wardrobe packs: install a 3D Avatar Maker wardrobe pack and its clothes appear in the Clothes tab, each with a line saying which pack it's from and who made it. The first two are Everyday Women's Clothes and Everyday Men's Clothes and Suits.
- A saved avatar names the makers of any credited pack clothes it wears.

## 0.1.02 — 2026-11-23

- Real nails on every finger and toe: short, medium, long or none, and toenails on or off, in any colour. Gloves and shoes hide them.
- 23 more skins, among them freckled, ginger, bronzed, Eurasian, tattooed and made-up ones.
- 15 more eye pictures: amber, hazel, sapphire and other natural colours, cat's eyes, reptile and dragon eyes, and a trial anime eye.
- Four brow styles and two lash styles with fine, natural hairs.
- 29 more sliders for the cheeks, ears, nose, arms and hands, such as jowls, a bulbous nose and knobbly knuckles. A new avatar starts with truer ears, nose and hands.

## 0.1.01 — 2026-11-23

- Nails are now a part of their own on every finger and toe, so a nail colour has a clean edge.
- 46 new face sliders in eight groups (eyes, brows, nose, mouth, jaw and chin, cheeks, ears, head), with ready-made shapes for each, such as hooded eyes, a Roman nose or an underbite.
- 15 new body sliders for the arms, legs, torso and hips, and her bust.
- Races are presets on the same body: choosing one sets race sliders you can mix and adjust, plus height and limbs. Horns, tusks, a tail and beards are accessories anyone can wear.
- A first Anime proportions style, and clean-edged lip colour.

## 0.1.00 — 2026-11-22

- First version: a Home tab where you build a 3D avatar for a character or persona and save it as a GLB file for the 3D Avatars agent.
- Male and female bodies in eight races, with build, face and proportion sliders, three ages, skin and eye pictures or colours of your own, 14 hair styles, and 78 pieces of clothing and armour.
- Save the look as a small file, or open a saved avatar again to keep editing it.
- The face doesn't move yet, so in a chat the avatar nods while it talks.
