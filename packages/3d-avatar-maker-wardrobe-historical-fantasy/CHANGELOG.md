# 3D Avatar Maker: Historical and Fantasy Clothes release notes

## 0.1.00 — 2026-11-23

- First version: 17 pieces of clothing for 3D Avatar Maker's Clothes tab: a monk's robe with two hoods, a medieval dress, three goddess dresses, a peasant blouse, a tunic with a sash, a maid's headband, a veil, a top hat, a pith helmet, a brass circlet, and medieval boots, riding boots and felt elf shoes.
- From the MakeHuman community. The robe and hoods are CC0; the rest are CC BY 4.0, and a saved avatar names the maker of each one it wears. Needs 3D Avatar Maker 0.1.03 or later.
